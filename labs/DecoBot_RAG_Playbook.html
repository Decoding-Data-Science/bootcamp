DecoBot RAG Playbook

A RAG bot is only as good as the document behind it. This playbook runs on the real DDS Leave Policy v2. You will see how chunking changes what the bot finds, why only the latest policy may be searchable, how RAG fails, and who must keep the knowledge current.

Decoding Data Science. The search here is a simple word-match model in your browser, so you can see the idea without a key. Real RAG uses embeddings. Policy text: DDS Leave Policy v2, DDS-HR-POL-001 (synthetic).
